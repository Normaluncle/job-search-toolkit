Notion Job Application Tracker Template

Once you've sent more than a dozen applications, it gets hard to remember who you applied to, what happened, and who you still owe a follow-up. This template keeps every application in one Notion database, so the next step is always written down.

What's in the free Lite version

Every application is one row with these properties:

Property What it holds
Company The employer
Role The position you applied for
Status Wishlist / Applied / Assessment / Interviewing / Offer / Rejected
Next Action The one thing you need to do next, e.g. "Send follow-up email"
Next Action Date When you need to do it
Days since applied Counted automatically from the date you applied

It comes with two views:

  • Pipeline Board: one column per status, so you can drag an application from Applied to Interviewing.
  • Calendar: every Next Action Date on a calendar, so follow-ups don't slip.

A simple rule that keeps the board clean: if two follow-ups get no reply, mark it Rejected and move on.

Get the free Lite version. Open it and click Duplicate. It works on the free Notion plan.

  • Weekly review page
  • Companies database that counts your applications per company

FAQ

Do I need a paid Notion plan? No. Both versions work on the free plan.

Can I change the statuses? Yes. It's a normal Notion database, so you can rename or add options.
