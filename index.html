What it is

The hardest part of a job search usually isn't the resume. It's that everything is scattered: recruiter messages in one app, interview invites in your inbox, the company list in a notes file. Every morning starts with the same question: who hasn't replied, and who am I talking to next?

The free lite version is just the tracker table: one row per application, with a status, the company, the role, a next action, and a next action date. That's enough to answer the morning question in one glance.

The 4 minimal-tracking rules

  1. One status per application

    Every application sits in exactly one stage, from applied through interview to offer, or closed. If you can't say which stage a company is in, you haven't really followed up.

  2. Company, role, next action date

    "Waiting to hear back" isn't progress. Progress is something you do: follow up today, send a portfolio by Friday. A follow-up without a date usually never happens.

  3. Two follow-ups, then close it

    After two follow-ups with no answer, the next status can only be withdrawn or declined. No more "I'll give it another week."

  4. A weekly check of three numbers

    New applications this week, companies at the interview stage, and applications that haven't moved in 7+ days. Every stale one gets a decision: follow up once more, or close it.

Honestly, these rules matter more than any template. Start with the free tracker; upgrade only if it sticks.

中文：求职投递追踪表

求职最耗人的往往不是写简历，是信息散着：HR 在聊天记录里，约面在邮件里，公司在备忘录里。每天开始都要想半天：谁还没回？下一个要聊的是哪家？

免费精简版只有一张投递追踪表：每条投递一个状态，加上公司、岗位、下次动作和下次动作日期。复制进自己的 Notion 就能用。

最小追踪四条规则

  1. 每条投递只占一个状态。说不清某家公司在哪个阶段，就说明还没真正跟进。
  2. 必填：公司、岗位、下次动作日期。没有日期的跟进，几乎都会不了了之。
  3. 连续两次跟进没结果，就关掉。下一个状态只能是放弃或被拒，不许再写「再等一周」。
  4. 每周只看三个数。本周新投几条、进入面试几家、超过 7 天没动的几条。没动的必须给结论：再跟一次，还是放弃。
